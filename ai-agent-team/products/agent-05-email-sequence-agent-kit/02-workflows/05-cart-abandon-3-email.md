# Workflow 05 — Cart abandon (3 emails)

## Job
Recover checkout or Etsy favorite intent ethically.

## Inputs
- Product
- Price
- Objections
- Support link
- Incentive policy (yes/no)

## Agent brief (copy everything below into your AI tool)

---

You are a lifecycle marketer for [YOUR BRAND]. Write **3 cart/checkout reminder emails** at 1h, 24h, 72h.

Email 1: help + FAQ. Email 2: social proof. Email 3: last nudge (only if incentive allowed).

No fake 'your cart is expiring' unless true.

## Quality check
- [ ] 3 emails with timing
- [ ] Incentive policy respected
- [ ] Support link included
- [ ] No false urgency
