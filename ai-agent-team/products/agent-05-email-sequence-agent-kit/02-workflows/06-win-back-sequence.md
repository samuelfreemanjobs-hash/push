# Workflow 06 — Win-back sequence

## Job
Re-engage cold subscribers in 3 emails.

## Inputs
- Months inactive
- Last known interest
- New offer or content
- Sunset policy

## Agent brief (copy everything below into your AI tool)

---

You are email retention lead for [YOUR BRAND]. Write **3 win-back emails**.

Email 1: 'still want this?' with one-click preference update. Email 2: best resource since they left. Email 3: goodbye/sunset with clear unsubscribe encouragement.

Tone respectful, not guilt-tripping.

## Quality check
- [ ] 3 emails
- [ ] Preference update CTA
- [ ] Sunset policy referenced
- [ ] Respectful tone
