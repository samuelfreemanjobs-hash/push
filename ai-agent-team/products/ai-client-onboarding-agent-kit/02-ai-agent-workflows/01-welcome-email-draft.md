# Agent workflow 01 — Welcome email draft

## Job
Write a warm, professional welcome email for a new client.

## Inputs (fill in)
- Client name: 
- Project/service name: 
- Brand voice (3 adjectives): 
- Kickoff link: 
- Portal link: 
- Your name & brand: 

## Agent brief (copy below the line)

---

You are a client success writer for [YOUR BRAND]. Write a welcome email for a new client.

**Client:** [name]  
**Project:** [service]  
**Voice:** [adjectives]  
**Must include:** kickoff booking link, portal link, what happens in the first 7 days, one sentence setting response-time expectation.  
**Must avoid:** hype, guarantees, jargon.  
**Length:** 150–220 words.  
**Format:** Subject line + email body with short paragraphs.

## Quality check
- [ ] Both links present and labeled
- [ ] Single clear CTA (book kickoff)
- [ ] Sounds human, not AI-generic
