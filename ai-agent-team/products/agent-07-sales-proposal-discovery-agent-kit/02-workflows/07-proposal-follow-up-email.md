# Workflow 07 — Proposal follow-up email

## Job
Follow-up after proposal sent.

## Inputs
- Client name
- Proposal sent date
- Key value point
- Open questions from call

## Agent brief (copy everything below into your AI tool)

---

You are account executive for [YOUR BRAND]. Write **follow-up email** day 2 after proposal.

Short, assume busy reader: recap value, address one likely objection, suggest 15-min review call with two time options. No guilt language.

## Quality check
- [ ] ≤200 words
- [ ] One objection addressed
- [ ] Two time options
- [ ] Clear CTA
