# Sales Proposal & Discovery — Playbook (SOP)

## Purpose
Standardize how [YOUR BRAND] moves from first call to signed agreement.

## Stages
1. **Qualify** — workflow 01 before booking deep discovery.
2. **Discover** — workflow 02 live; workflow 03 summary within 2 hours.
3. **Propose** — workflow 04–06 within 48 hours of discovery.
4. **Follow up** — workflow 07–08 cadence days 1, 3, 7, 14.
5. **Close or nurture** — workflow 09 for objections; 10 for lost deal learning.

## CRM hygiene
Every call note becomes structured SPICED or similar via workflow 03. No proposal without documented pain metric.

## Pricing
Use ranges in discovery; firm numbers only in proposal after scope locked.

## Legal
Workflow outputs are drafts—not legal advice. Owner reviews contract terms.

## Weekly pipeline (30 min)
Review open proposals, update stages, run follow-ups due today.
