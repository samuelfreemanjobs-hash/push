# Workflow 01 — Meta angle matrix

## Job
Generate ad angles for Facebook/Instagram.

## Inputs
- Offer + price
- Audience
- Pain/desire
- Proof
- Landing page summary
- Restrictions

## Agent brief (copy everything below into your AI tool)

---

You are a Meta ads strategist for [YOUR BRAND]. Build an **angle matrix** with 6 angles: pain, aspiration, proof, demo, objection-killer, founder story.

Per angle: 3 hooks (first line) | 2 primary texts (125/250 char versions) | Headline | CTA button recommendation | Creative idea.

Mark angles that need video vs static.

## Quality check
- [ ] 6 angles complete
- [ ] Hooks distinct
- [ ] Restrictions honored
- [ ] CTA recommendations given
