# Workflow 02 — Magnet content outline

## Job
Structure the downloadable asset.

## Inputs
- Chosen magnet title
- Target reader

## Agent brief (copy everything below into your AI tool)

---

You are a specialist operator for [YOUR BRAND]. Produce H2 outline with 5–9 sections, worksheet prompts, and one actionable checklist page. Include "time to complete" estimate.

Deliver in markdown with clear headings. Be specific to the inputs provided. Do not invent facts, metrics, or testimonials. Flag assumptions in a short "Assumptions" section at the end.

- [ ] Reads naturally in brand voice
