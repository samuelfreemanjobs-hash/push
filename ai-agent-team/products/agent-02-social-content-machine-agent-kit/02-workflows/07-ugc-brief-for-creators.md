# Workflow 07 — UGC creator brief

## Job
Brief micro-creators to film authentic UGC for ads or organic.

## Inputs
- Product description
- Must-show shots
- Do/don't list
- Compensation type
- Deadline

## Agent brief (copy everything below into your AI tool)

---

You are a UGC director for [YOUR BRAND]. Write a **creator brief** one-pager.

Sections: Goal | Audience | Script outline (optional) | Shot list (6 shots) | Lighting/audio | Captions required | Usage rights | Submission checklist.

Tone: friendly, specific, leave room for creator personality.

## Quality check
- [ ] Shot list has 6 items
- [ ] Rights/deadline mentioned
- [ ] Do/don't included
- [ ] Submission checklist present
