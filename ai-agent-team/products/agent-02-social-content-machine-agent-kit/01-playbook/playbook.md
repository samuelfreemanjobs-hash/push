# Social Content Machine — Playbook (SOP)

## Purpose
Run a predictable weekly batch so [YOUR BRAND] publishes daily without last-minute scrambling.

## Weekly batch (2 hours)
**Plan (20 min):** Pick weekly theme tied to one product or pain point. Run workflow 01.

**Create (60 min):** Run workflows 02–05 for your top 2 platforms. Aim for 5–7 posts per platform.

**Engage (20 min):** Queue workflow 06 questions and 08 hashtag sets. Schedule via Meta Business Suite, Later, or Pinterest native.

**Repurpose (20 min):** Workflow 09 turns one long asset into micro-posts.

## 30-day cycle
Week 1: education | Week 2: proof/stories | Week 3: product spotlight | Week 4: community/UGC.

## Quality bar
Every post needs one clear CTA, one visual note, and brand voice check. No engagement bait that violates platform rules.

## Metrics review (monthly)
Top 5 posts by saves/clicks → clone format next month. Kill formats with <1% engagement twice in a row.

## Roles
Owner approves calendar; AI drafts; human films/photographs templates noted in each brief.
