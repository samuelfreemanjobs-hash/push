# Marketing Planner — Playbook (SOP)

## Purpose
Keep [YOUR BRAND] marketing proactive: one source of truth in **Marketing-Command-Center.xlsx** plus AI-assisted briefs.

## Weekly ops (Friday, 60 min)
1. Export last week's numbers into the KPI tab.
2. Run workflow **weekly-marketing-priorities** for next week.
3. Update campaign tab statuses (idea/active/done).
4. Assign owners and due dates.

## Monthly (first Monday, 90 min)
Run **ninety-day-campaign-roadmap** and **channel-experiment-queue**. Archive completed campaigns to `/marketing/archive`.

## Launch protocol
For any launch: workflow **campaign-brief-one-pager** → creative tasks → workflow **launch-day-checklist** → workflow **post-launch-retro** within 7 days.

## Meeting cadence
15-min daily standup optional; 30-min weekly marketing sync mandatory when more than one person touches campaigns.

## Quality bar
Every active campaign has: goal metric, owner, start/end, offer, primary channel, backup channel.

## Tooling
Spreadsheet is system of record; AI drafts narrative sections you paste into Notes columns.
