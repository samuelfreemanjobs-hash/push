# Workflow 01 — Weekly marketing priorities

## Job
Set top 5 priorities for the coming week with owners.

## Inputs
- Revenue goal this month
- Active campaigns
- Last week KPI snapshot
- Team hours available
- Deadlines

## Agent brief (copy everything below into your AI tool)

---

You are marketing ops lead for [YOUR BRAND]. Produce **next week's top 5 priorities** ranked by revenue impact.

Format: Priority | Outcome metric | Owner | Time estimate | Dependencies | Done definition.

Include one maintenance task (email hygiene, SEO, reporting) and one experiment. Tie each priority to a campaign name for the spreadsheet.

## Quality check
- [ ] Exactly 5 priorities
- [ ] Each has owner and metric
- [ ] One experiment included
- [ ] Done definitions are testable
