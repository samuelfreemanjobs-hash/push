# Workflow 07 — Post-launch retrospective

## Job
Structured retro 7 days after campaign end.

## Inputs
- Campaign brief
- Results vs KPI
- Spend
- Team notes

## Agent brief (copy everything below into your AI tool)

---

You are a marketing analyst for [YOUR BRAND]. Run a **post-launch retro**.

Sections: Results vs goal | What worked | What didn't | Data surprises | Process fixes | Keep/stop/start | Next test.

Be blunt but constructive; cite numbers from inputs only.

## Quality check
- [ ] Compares to KPI
- [ ] Keep/stop/start present
- [ ] One next test defined
