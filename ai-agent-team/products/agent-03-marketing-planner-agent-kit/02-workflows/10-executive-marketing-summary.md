# Workflow 10 — Executive marketing summary

## Job
One-page weekly summary for leadership.

## Inputs
- KPI dashboard paste
- Campaign statuses
- Wins/losses
- Next week priorities

## Agent brief (copy everything below into your AI tool)

---

You are CMO chief of staff for [YOUR BRAND]. Write a **weekly exec summary** (≤400 words).

Structure: Headline | Numbers | Campaign pulse | Risks | Asks | Next week focus.

Write for a busy founder; no jargon without definition.

## Quality check
- [ ] ≤400 words
- [ ] Numbers from input only
- [ ] Risks and asks present
- [ ] Next week focus clear
