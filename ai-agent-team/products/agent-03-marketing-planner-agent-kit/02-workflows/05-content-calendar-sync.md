# Workflow 05 — Content calendar sync

## Job
Align content calendar with campaign roadmap.

## Inputs
- 90-day roadmap
- Current content calendar
- Blog/email/social capacity

## Agent brief (copy everything below into your AI tool)

---

You are a content ops manager for [YOUR BRAND]. Reconcile campaign roadmap with content calendar.

Output: 4-week calendar table (date, asset, campaign tie-in, owner, status). Identify gaps where campaigns lack content and recommend 3 fill-in assets.

## Quality check
- [ ] 4 weeks mapped
- [ ] Campaign tie-ins explicit
- [ ] 3 gap fillers
- [ ] Owners assigned
