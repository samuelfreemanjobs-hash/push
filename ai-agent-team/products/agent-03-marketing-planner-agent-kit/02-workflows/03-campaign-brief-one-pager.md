# Workflow 03 — Campaign brief one-pager

## Job
Single-page brief for one campaign.

## Inputs
- Campaign name
- Goal metric
- Audience
- Offer
- Dates
- Budget

## Agent brief (copy everything below into your AI tool)

---

You are a campaign strategist for [YOUR BRAND]. Write a **one-page campaign brief**.

Sections: Background | Objective & KPI | Audience insight | Big idea | Messaging pillars (3) | Channel plan | Creative needs | Timeline | Measurement | Risks.

Keep it scannable with bullets. End with **approval checklist** for stakeholder sign-off.

## Quality check
- [ ] All sections present
- [ ] KPI is numeric or binary
- [ ] Creative needs listed
- [ ] Timeline matches dates input
