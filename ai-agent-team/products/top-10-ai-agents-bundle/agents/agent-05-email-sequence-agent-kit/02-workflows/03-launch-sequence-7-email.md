# Workflow 03 — Launch sequence (7 emails)

## Job
Product launch sequence with ethical urgency.

## Inputs
- Offer name/price/deadline
- Bonuses
- Proof (testimonials)
- Objections (3)
- Cart link

## Agent brief (copy everything below into your AI tool)

---

You are a launch copywriter for [YOUR BRAND]. Write **7 emails** over 10 days for a product launch.

Map: seed problem → methodology → case study → offer reveal → FAQ → urgency → last call.

Subjects must not be deceptive. Include plain-text link line. Note which email to skip for existing customers.

## Quality check
- [ ] 7 emails mapped to days
- [ ] FAQ email addresses objections
- [ ] Deadline honest
- [ ] Customer skip note
