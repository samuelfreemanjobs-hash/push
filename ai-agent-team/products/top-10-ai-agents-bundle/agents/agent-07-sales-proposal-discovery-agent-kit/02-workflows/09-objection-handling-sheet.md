# Workflow 09 — Objection handling sheet

## Job
Responses for top objections on this deal.

## Inputs
- Objections heard (3–5)
- Offer
- Proof points

## Agent brief (copy everything below into your AI tool)

---

You are sales coach for [YOUR BRAND]. For each objection, give **acknowledge | reframe | proof | question back** script.

Include 'walk away gracefully' line if objection is fundamental.
