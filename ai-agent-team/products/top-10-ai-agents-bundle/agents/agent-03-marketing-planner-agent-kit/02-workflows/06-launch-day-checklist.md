# Workflow 06 — Launch day checklist

## Job
Hour-by-hour launch checklist for a campaign.

## Inputs
- Launch datetime
- Channels launching
- Assets list
- Support contact
- Rollback plan

## Agent brief (copy everything below into your AI tool)

---

You are a launch manager for [YOUR BRAND]. Create **launch day checklist** T-24h to T+24h.

Group by: email, social, paid, site, ops. Include verification steps (links, coupons, tracking UTMs). Add war-room roles and escalation if metrics dip.

## Quality check
- [ ] T-24 to T+24 coverage
- [ ] UTM/check links included
- [ ] Roles assigned
- [ ] Rollback mentioned
