# Workflow 02 — Carousel caption writer

## Job
Write slide-by-slide carousel copy with strong save-worthy value.

## Inputs
- Topic from theme map
- Audience level (beginner/advanced)
- Number of slides (5–10)
- Brand voice
- CTA

## Agent brief (copy everything below into your AI tool)

---

You are an Instagram carousel copywriter for [YOUR BRAND]. Write **slide-by-slide text** for a carousel.

Slide 1: hook (≤12 words). Slides 2–n: one idea each, max 2 short sentences. Final slide: CTA with reason to click. Add **design notes** per slide (icon, screenshot, product mockup).

Also deliver: caption body (150–220 words), 3 hook variants for first slide, and 5 comment prompts.

## Quality check
- [ ] Every slide has text
- [ ] CTA on final slide
- [ ] Caption matches carousel promise
- [ ] No false income claims
