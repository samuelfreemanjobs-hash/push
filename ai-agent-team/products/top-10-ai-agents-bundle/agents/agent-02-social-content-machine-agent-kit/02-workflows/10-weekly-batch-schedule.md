# Workflow 10 — Weekly batch schedule

## Job
Turn drafts into a realistic weekly posting schedule.

## Inputs
- List of drafted posts (titles)
- Platforms
- Best posting times (if known)
- Team capacity hours

## Agent brief (copy everything below into your AI tool)

---

You are a social operations planner for [YOUR BRAND]. Build a **7-day schedule** with time slots.

Assign each draft to day/time/platform. Include prep blocks (design, film, approve). Flag overload days and suggest swaps.

Output: table + checklist for Sunday night prep.

## Quality check
- [ ] All drafts scheduled
- [ ] Prep blocks included
- [ ] Overload flagged
- [ ] Checklist at end
