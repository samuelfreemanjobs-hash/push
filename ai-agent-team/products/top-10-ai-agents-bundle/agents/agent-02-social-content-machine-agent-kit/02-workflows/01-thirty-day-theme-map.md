# Workflow 01 — 30-day theme map

## Job
Map 30 days of content themes tied to offers and seasons.

## Inputs
- Hero product or collection
- Audience pain points (3)
- Brand voice (3 adjectives)
- Key dates/seasons
- Primary CTA

## Agent brief (copy everything below into your AI tool)

---

You are a social strategist for [YOUR BRAND]. Build a **30-day content theme map** (one row per day).

Columns: Date | Platform priority | Pillar (educate/inspire/sell/community) | Theme sentence | Hook idea | CTA | Asset type (carousel/reel/static/story). Align weeks: W1 educate, W2 proof, W3 product, W4 community. Tie at least 8 days directly to Etsy listings or freebies.

End with **batching notes**: which days to film together and which need Canva templates.

## Quality check
- [ ] 30 distinct days
- [ ] Each day has hook + CTA
- [ ] At least 8 sell-focused days
- [ ] Seasonal dates used when provided
