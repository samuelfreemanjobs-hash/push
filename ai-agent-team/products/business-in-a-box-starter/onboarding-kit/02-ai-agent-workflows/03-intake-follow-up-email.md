# Agent workflow 03 — Intake follow-up email

## Job
Draft a polite follow-up listing missing intake items.

## Inputs
- Client first name: 
- Missing items (list): 
- Original due date:

## Agent brief

---

Write a follow-up email from [YOUR NAME] at [YOUR BRAND] to [client]. They have not submitted: [items]. Original ask date: [date]. Milestone affected: [milestone].
