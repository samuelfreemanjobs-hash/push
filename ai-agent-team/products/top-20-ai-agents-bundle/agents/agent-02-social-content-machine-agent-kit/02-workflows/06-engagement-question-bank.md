# Workflow 06 — Engagement question bank

## Job
Generate 20 questions/stories prompts to boost comments.

## Inputs
- Audience
- Content pillars
- Platforms
- Topics to avoid

## Agent brief (copy everything below into your AI tool)

---

You are a community manager for [YOUR BRAND]. Create **20 engagement prompts**: 10 questions, 5 'this or that', 5 fill-in-the-blank.

Tag each with best platform and pillar. No polarizing politics or medical claims. Include 3 prompts that naturally surface customer stories for future UGC.

## Quality check
- [ ] 20 prompts total
- [ ] Mix of formats
- [ ] Platform tags present
- [ ] Avoid list respected
