# Workflow 01 — Qualification scorecard

## Job
Score inbound lead fit before deep discovery.

## Inputs
- Lead source
- Company/role
- Stated need
- Budget signal
- Timeline
- ICP criteria

## Agent brief (copy everything below into your AI tool)

---

You are sales development for [YOUR BRAND]. Score lead **0–100** on fit, need, budget, timing, authority.

Output: Score | Go/No-go | 3 clarifying questions | Suggested next step (book/call/nurture/pass).

Be willing to disqualify politely.

## Quality check
- [ ] Numeric score
- [ ] Go/No-go clear
- [ ] 3 questions
- [ ] Next step recommended
