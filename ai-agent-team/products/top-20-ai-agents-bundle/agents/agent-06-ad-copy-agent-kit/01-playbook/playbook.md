# Ad Copy — Playbook (SOP)

## Purpose
Produce disciplined ad creative tests for [YOUR BRAND] without random one-off copy.

## Weekly cycle (90 min)
Monday: pick single offer + angle hypothesis. Tuesday: run workflows 01–03. Wednesday: upload variants. Thursday: monitor 48h leading indicators. Friday: document winners in testing log.

## Testing rules
Change one variable at a time when possible (hook OR visual OR audience). Minimum spend threshold before killing (set in sheet).

## Platform notes
**Meta:** 3–5 primary texts × 3 headlines per ad set. **Google RSA:** 8–15 headlines, 4 descriptions, pin sparingly.

## Compliance
No before/after body claims unless allowed. Disclose digital product nature. Follow ad platform policies for income claims.

## Creative handoff
Pair every copy set with workflow **ad-creative-brief-for-designer** before design starts.

## Archive
Store winning ads in `/ads/winners` with date and metrics snapshot.
