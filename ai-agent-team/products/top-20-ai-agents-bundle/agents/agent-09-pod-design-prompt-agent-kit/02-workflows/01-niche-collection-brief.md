# Workflow 01 — Niche collection brief

## Job
Plan a 8-design POD collection for a niche.

## Inputs
- Niche/audience
- Product types
- Season
- Competitor style notes
- Banned topics

## Agent brief (copy everything below into your AI tool)

---

You are POD merchandiser for [YOUR BRAND]. Create **collection brief**: name | buyer persona | 8 design concepts (title + one-line concept) | shared palette (hex) | typography style | mockup notes.

Concepts must be original, niche-specific, not generic 'live laugh love' unless ironic per brief.

## Quality check
- [ ] 8 concepts
- [ ] Palette hex codes
- [ ] Persona clear
- [ ] Banned topics avoided
