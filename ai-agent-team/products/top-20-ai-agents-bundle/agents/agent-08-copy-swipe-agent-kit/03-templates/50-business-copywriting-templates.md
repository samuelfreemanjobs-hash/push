# 50 business copywriting templates — [YOUR BRAND]

Fill brackets, then paste into email, site, or AI for polish.

## Offers & positioning (1–10)

**1. One-line value prop**  
We help [audience] achieve [outcome] without [pain] using [method].

**2. Elevator pitch (30 sec)**  
You know how [audience] struggle with [problem]? We built [product/service] so they can [outcome] in [timeframe]. Unlike [alternative], we [differentiator].

**3. Homepage hero**  
Headline: [Outcome] for [audience]  
Sub: [How you deliver] — without [objection].  
CTA: [Primary action]

**4. Feature → benefit**  
[Feature] so you can [benefit], which means [life/business result].

**5. Risk reversal**  
Try [offer] for [period]. If you don't [specific result], [guarantee per your policy].

**6. Scarcity (ethical)**  
We onboard [N] clients per month to protect quality. [N] slots left for [month].

**7. Social proof blurb**  
"[Quote]" — [Name], [Role], [Company]

**8. Comparison (us vs DIY)**  
DIY: [pain list]. With us: [outcome list]. You stay focused on [core job].

**9. Pricing page intro**  
Pick the plan that matches [stage]. Every tier includes [baseline]. Upgrade when you need [trigger].

**10. Footer tagline**  
[YOUR BRAND] — [outcome] for [audience].

## Email (11–22)

**11. Cold outreach subject**  
Quick idea for [their company]'s [area]

**12. Cold outreach body**  
Hi [Name] — noticed [specific observation]. We help [similar companies] with [outcome]. Worth a 15-min chat? [link]

**13. Nurture email #1**  
Subject: The one metric [audience] ignore  
Body: [Story + tip + soft CTA]

**14. Nurture email #2 — case study**  
How [client type] went from [before] to [after] in [time].

**15. Launch announcement**  
Subject: It's live: [offer name]  
What's included, who it's for, link, deadline if any.

**16. Cart / inquiry follow-up**  
Still thinking about [offer]? Here's [FAQ answer / bonus]. Reply with questions.

**17. Re-engagement**  
We haven't connected since [date]. Still working on [goal]? If yes — [CTA]. If not — [unsubscribe-friendly close].

**18. Referral ask**  
Know someone who needs [outcome]? Forward this — they get [incentive], you get [thank you].

**19. Testimonial request**  
Would you share 2–3 sentences on [specific result]? Reply here; we'll send a draft you can edit.

**20. Invoice / payment reminder (friendly)**  
Invoice [#] for [project] is due [date]. Link: [url]. Questions welcome.

**21. Onboarding welcome**  
(See onboarding kit — or) Welcome! Next: [3 steps].

**22. Newsletter framework**  
Hook → Insight → Example → Action → P.S. with CTA

## Social (23–32)

**23. LinkedIn hook**  
Most [audience] don't need more [tactic]. They need [system/outcome].

**24. LinkedIn story post**  
Last [timeframe] we [action]. Result: [metric]. Here's what we'd do differently: [lesson]

**25. LinkedIn carousel slide 1**  
[Number] mistakes [audience] make with [topic]

**26. Instagram caption**  
[Hook line]  
[3 bullets]  
CTA: Link in bio for [offer]  
Hashtags: [5 relevant]

**27. Pinterest description**  
[Keyword-rich sentence]. Includes [what's in download]. For [audience]. Click to get [outcome].

**28. Twitter/X thread opener**  
I spent [time] studying [topic]. [Number] threads 🧵

**29. Comment engagement**  
Great point on [detail]. We saw similar when [brief example]. Did you try [tactic]?

**30. Poll**  
What's blocking your [goal] most right now? A) … B) … C) …

**31. Behind-the-scenes**  
Building [asset] for [client type] — here's the [framework/tool] we use.

**32. User-generated prompt**  
Tag a founder who needs to hear: [short advice].

## Ads & landing (33–40)

**33. Meta ad primary text**  
[Problem] → [Solution] → [Proof] → [CTA]

**34. Google ad headline 1**  
[Outcome] | [YOUR BRAND]

**35. Google ad description**  
[Method] for [audience]. [CTA]. [Trust element].

**36. Landing page — problem section**  
You're not alone if [symptom 1], [symptom 2], [symptom 3].

**37. Landing page — solution**  
[Product] gives you [deliverable 1], [2], [3] so you can [outcome].

**38. FAQ answer**  
Q: [objection]  
A: [direct answer] + [proof or policy]

**39. Thank-you page**  
You're in. Check [email] for [next step]. While you wait: [secondary CTA].

**40. Retargeting ad**  
Still [desire]? [Offer reminder]. [Urgency if true].

## Sales & proposals (41–50)

**41. Discovery call opener**  
Thanks for making time. Goal today: understand [goal] and see if we're a fit. Sound good?

**42. Discovery question bank**  
What does success look like in [timeframe]? What's been tried? What's the cost of doing nothing?

**43. Proposal executive summary**  
[Client] needs [outcome]. We recommend [scope] over [timeline] at [investment]. Expected impact: [metrics].

**44. Scope bullet**  
**In scope:** [list]  
**Out of scope:** [list]

**45. Objection — price**  
Compared to [cost of problem], this is [frame]. We can phase work: [option].

**46. Objection — timing**  
If not now, what changes by [date]? We can start with [smaller phase].

**47. Closing email**  
Based on our call, attached is [proposal]. Next step: [action] by [date]. Questions?

**48. Upsell**  
Now that [phase 1] is live, clients often add [phase 2] to get [outcome].

**49. Renewal**  
Your [period] with us ends [date]. Results so far: [summary]. Continue with [plan]?

**50. Thank-you / milestone**  
We hit [milestone] together. Thank you for [specific behavior]. Next up: [step].
