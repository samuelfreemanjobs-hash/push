# Workflow 08 — Email QA & compliance

## Job
Final QA before sending any campaign.

## Inputs
- Full email draft
- Jurisdiction (US/EU)
- Physical address available?
- Link list

## Agent brief (copy everything below into your AI tool)

---

You are email compliance QA for [YOUR BRAND]. Audit the draft.

Checklist: subject honesty, unsubscribe, address, link validity placeholders, spam words, accessibility (alt text note), mobile length.

Output Pass/Fail with fix list prioritized.

## Quality check
- [ ] Pass/Fail stated
- [ ] Fix list prioritized
- [ ] Unsubscribe mentioned
- [ ] Links listed for verification
