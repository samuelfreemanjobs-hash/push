# Workflow 01 — Welcome sequence (5 emails)

## Job
Draft 5-email welcome over 7 days.

## Inputs
- Lead magnet description
- Audience
- Core offer + price
- Brand story (3 bullets)
- Voice
- ESP merge tags

## Agent brief (copy everything below into your AI tool)

---

You are an email copywriter for [YOUR BRAND]. Write a **5-email welcome sequence** over 7 days.

For each email: Send day | Subject (3 variants) | Preview text | Body (markdown) | CTA | Goal metric.

Arc: deliver asset → founder story → quick tutorial → social proof → soft pitch with guarantee framing. Use merge tags like {{first_name}} only if provided.

## Quality check
- [ ] 5 emails with send days
- [ ] 3 subjects each
- [ ] Lead magnet delivered email 1
- [ ] Soft pitch only by email 5
