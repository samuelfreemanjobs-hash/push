# Workflow 04 — Subject line battery

## Job
Generate 30 subject lines for testing.

## Inputs
- Email summary
- Audience awareness level
- Tone
- Words to avoid

## Agent brief (copy everything below into your AI tool)

---

You are an email CTR specialist for [YOUR BRAND]. Write **30 subject lines** grouped by angle: curiosity, benefit, proof, direct, question.

Each ≤50 characters where possible. Flag 5 as 'high risk spam' to avoid. Pair top 5 with preview text.

## Quality check
- [ ] 30 subjects
- [ ] 5 categories
- [ ] Preview text for top 5
- [ ] Spam flags explained
