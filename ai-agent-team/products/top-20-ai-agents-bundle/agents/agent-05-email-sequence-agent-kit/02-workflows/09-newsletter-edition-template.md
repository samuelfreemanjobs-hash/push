# Workflow 09 — Newsletter edition template

## Job
Recurring newsletter structure with sections.

## Inputs
- Theme of week
- 3 content links
- Product spotlight
- Personal note seed
- Voice

## Agent brief (copy everything below into your AI tool)

---

You are newsletter editor for [YOUR BRAND]. Write one **newsletter edition** using fixed sections: Opener | Tip of the week | Link trio | Spotlight | CTA | Sign-off.

600–900 words max. Skimmable subheads.

## Quality check
- [ ] All sections present
- [ ] Word count in range
- [ ] One primary CTA
- [ ] Links from inputs used
