# Workflow 02 — 90-day campaign roadmap

## Job
Lay out campaigns for the next quarter.

## Inputs
- Annual goal
- Product/offer list
- Seasonality
- Budget range
- Channels

## Agent brief (copy everything below into your AI tool)

---

You are a campaign planner for [YOUR BRAND]. Build a **90-day roadmap** by week.

Columns: Week | Campaign | Objective | Channel mix | Offer | Success metric | Risk.

Balance launch weeks with nurture weeks. Note email, paid, organic, and partnerships. Flag resource conflicts.

## Quality check
- [ ] Covers 13 weeks
- [ ] Each week has objective
- [ ] Channel mix present
- [ ] Risks noted
