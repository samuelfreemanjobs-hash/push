# Workflow 04 — Channel experiment queue

## Job
Prioritize 5 marketing experiments with hypotheses.

## Inputs
- Channels in use
- Recent wins/failures
- Budget cap
- Skill constraints

## Agent brief (copy everything below into your AI tool)

---

You are a growth experimenter for [YOUR BRAND]. Propose **5 experiments** for the next 30 days.

Each: Hypothesis | Channel | Test design | Sample size/duration | Success metric | Kill criteria.

Prioritize ICE score (Impact, Confidence, Ease) 1–10.

## Quality check
- [ ] 5 experiments
- [ ] Kill criteria on each
- [ ] ICE scores included
- [ ] Budget cap respected
