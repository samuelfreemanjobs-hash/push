# 50 AI ops agent workflows — [YOUR BRAND]

Format: **Trigger** → paste **Agent brief** into your AI tool.

---

## Sales (1–10)

**1. Ideal customer profile refresh**  
*Trigger:* Quarterly planning  
*Brief:* Given we sell [offer] to [current clients], write an ICP with demographics, psychographics, 5 pain points, 5 buying triggers, and 3 disqualifiers. Table format.

**2. Discovery call summary**  
*Trigger:* After sales call  
*Brief:* Summarize notes into: Situation, Pain, Impact, Decision process, Next step. Notes: [paste]

**3. Proposal section draft**  
*Trigger:* Proposal in progress  
*Brief:* Write "Approach" section for [client] needing [outcome]. 250 words, phases, no jargon.

**4. Follow-up after no-show**  
*Trigger:* Prospect missed call  
*Brief:* 120-word email rescheduling, empathetic, two time options.

**5. Objection handler — budget**  
*Trigger:* "Too expensive"  
*Brief:* Give 3 responses: value reframe, phased scope, walk-away graceful. Product: [offer] price [X].

**6. Win/loss analysis**  
*Trigger:* Deal closed or lost  
*Brief:* From [notes], list likely reasons, lessons, one process change. Honest tone.

**7. LinkedIn connection note**  
*Trigger:* Outbound  
*Brief:* 300-char connection request for [name] at [company], reference [specific].

**8. RFP go/no-go**  
*Trigger:* RFP received  
*Brief:* Score RFP fit 1–10 on: margin, timeline, scope clarity, strategic value. Recommend bid or pass. RFP summary: [paste]

**9. Case study interview questions**  
*Trigger:* Client success  
*Brief:* 12 interview questions to extract before/after metrics for [industry] client.

**10. Pipeline review narrative**  
*Trigger:* Weekly sales meeting  
*Brief:* Turn pipeline data into 5-bullet exec summary: commits, risks, needed actions. Data: [paste]
