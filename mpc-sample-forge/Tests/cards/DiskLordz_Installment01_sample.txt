Sound design
- Electric piano: soften the attack, shorten the initial transient slightly, and keep the low end clean.
- Pad: attack around 500–800 ms; release around 1.5–2 seconds.
- Add chorus at approximately 15% wet.
- Add a quiet dotted-eighth delay and a short plate reverb.
- High-pass the layered pad around 120 Hz so it doesn't cloud the chord sample.
