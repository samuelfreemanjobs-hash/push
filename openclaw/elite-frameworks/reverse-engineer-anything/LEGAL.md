# Reverse Engineer Anything — DiskLordz policy

Use this toolkit **only** for:

- Interoperability you have legal right to study (your own binaries, OSS SDKs, permissive docs)
- File-format research documented in `templates/format-research-log.md`
- Defensive security on **your** staging services (`vercel-security-review/`)

**Do not use agents to:**

- Crack DRM, iLok, or third-party commercial plugins
- Distribute proprietary binaries or stolen samples
- Bypass license checks on paid products you do not own

Violations are out of scope for OpenClaw skills and void commercial kit clearance.
